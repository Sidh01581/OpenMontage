# WHO ATE MY LUNCH. — YouTube Short Plan

Draft for approval (Nov 25, 2026). Same Story Short format as BUSY. / THE PASSWORD NIGHTMARE. (9:16, ~44 s, comedy). Same cast, and **Priya leads** for the first time.

## Overview

Priya opens the office fridge. Her lunch, labeled **"PRIYA — DO NOT TOUCH"**, is gone. The picture drains to black-and-white, rain streaks the window, and the cheerful manager becomes a hard-boiled noir detective. She interrogates two suspects:
- **Dev** has crumbs all over his sweater. "I was eating my OWN lunch!" More crumbs fall out.
- **Theo** is sweating. "I didn't take the noodles." "Nobody said noodles." "…Lucky guess."

A trail of noodles leads under the desk, to **Mochi**, asleep in the empty container with a round belly and a noodle on his whiskers. "Case closed." Colour floods back. Then Dev asks: "Has anyone seen my sandwich?" Priya freezes mid-bite, holding Dev's sandwich. Hard cut to black.

**YouTube title:** When someone steals your lunch from the office fridge 🍜🕵️ #corporatelife

### Color plan

The film opens in normal office colour. When the lunch is found missing it drops to **black-and-white noir**: hard shadows, venetian-blind stripes and rain. The only colour left is the red "DO NOT TOUCH" label and the noodles. On "Case closed" colour floods back. The sandwich reveal is in full colour, then a hard cut to black.
