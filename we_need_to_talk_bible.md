# WE NEED TO TALK. — YouTube Short Plan

Source doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/NBXoYrN8DQ6naCWvXFnbDm (Nov 24, 2026)

## Overview

**WE NEED TO TALK.** is a ~42-second vertical Short. Theo's boss says "we need to talk tomorrow," a creature called What-If moves into his head, and he spends the whole night planning for unemployment. The next morning, wrecked and hopeless, he hears: "Congratulations, we want to discuss your hike."

Story Short format (same Theo, Mochi, look and sound approach as LATER.), squeezed into one punchy arc with a comedy twist instead of a lesson act.

**YouTube title:** "We need to talk tomorrow" 😰 #overthinking

### What changes for a Short

| Setting | Long-form Story Short | This Short |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame | 16:9, 1920×1080 | 9:16, 1080×1920 |
| Length | 3:10–3:40 | ~42 s (keep under 45 s) |
| Shots | ~50, cut every 2–4 s | 18, cut every 1.5–3 s |
| On-screen text | Title and closing line only | Hook caption at 0:00, plus big word-by-word captions throughout |
| Structure | 6 acts with a lesson | Hook, spiral, night, morning, twist, button gag |
| Ending | Moral line on black | Punchline that loops back to the start |

**Safe zones:** keep faces and captions in the middle 60% of the frame. YouTube's buttons cover the right edge and the bottom ~20%; the title and channel name cover the bottom-left.

## Sound

**No music.** Sound effects and voices carry the mood, like a sitcom cold open. Play the drama completely straight.

| Time | Sound bed | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:03 | Office hum, keyboard clicks | Normal day |
| 0:03–0:16 | One big orchestra sting, then What-If's buzzing grows louder with every shot; rain, train | Fake horror |
| 0:16–0:24 | Night silence, slow heartbeat, clock flips, overlapping "what if" whispers | Sleepless dread |
| 0:24–0:30 | Footsteps, slurp, then one clock tick and true silence at 0:29 | Maximum tension |
| 0:31–0:38 | Party popper, balloon pop with a deflating squeal, office applause | Release |
| 0:39–0:42 | Tiny buzz, eye-snap "ding", hard cut to silence | Punchline |

The sting, the buzz and the applause must be clear and loud enough to land on phone speakers, since there is no music underneath.

**Voices:** Piper, a different voice per character; pitch What-If up slightly with a light flutter.
