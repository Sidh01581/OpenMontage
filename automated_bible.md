# AUTOMATED. — YouTube Short Plan

Draft for approval (Nov 25, 2026). Same Story Short format as BUSY. / THE PASSWORD NIGHTMARE. (9:16, ~44 s, comedy). Same cast plus one new character: Dev's AI robot. The story is the user's; it replaces WHO ATE MY LUNCH., which is kept as a parked idea in `lunch_bible.md`.

## Overview

Dev installs an automation AI. A friendly little robot unfolds at his desk and starts doing his job at ten times his speed, while Dev leans back and scrolls his phone. He proudly tells Theo, "I automated my entire job. Now AI works for me." "So what do you do now?" "Nothing." Then the counter starts climbing: *Email sent. Email sent.* Priya storms in: the system has sent 500 emails, and it's emailing the entire company every 10 seconds. Dev calls it productivity. Priya keeps the robot: it doesn't take breaks, doesn't complain, and works 24/7. "But I created it!" "Exactly. That's your last successful contribution." Cut to the robot sitting in Dev's chair while Dev stands beside it holding a mop. Theo: "Congratulations, Dev. You automated yourself out of a job." Dev: "Can the robot automate my new job too?" Hard cut to black.

**YouTube title:** I automated my entire job 🤖😂 #corporatelife
