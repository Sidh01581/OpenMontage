# BUSY. — YouTube Short Plan

Revised Nov 24, 2026 to the user's script. Same Story Short format as SURE. (9:16, ~41 s, comedy).

## Overview

**BUSY.** is a ~41-second vertical Short. Theo narrates like a nature-documentary sports commentator while Dev, the busiest-looking man in the office, does absolutely nothing: he speed-walks with a coffee, carries random papers, checks his watch, nods gravely at coworkers and types like the keyboard owes him money. When the manager (Priya) walks by, he transforms into the busiest employee of the year. The moment she leaves, he's back to scrolling his phone. The end screen crowns him Employee of the Month, Master of Looking Busy.

**YouTube title:** That one coworker who's always "busy" ☕😂 #corporatelife
