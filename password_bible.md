# THE PASSWORD NIGHTMARE. — YouTube Short Plan

Draft for approval (Nov 25, 2026). Same Story Short format as BUSY. / QUICK SYNC. (9:16, ~45 s, comedy). Same cast. Replaces the QUICK SYNC. story (its files are kept, parked).

## Overview

Dev's password has expired. "Easy," he says. Then the password rules start, and each one is worse than the last: a capital letter, a number, a symbol, *can't contain the word "password"*, can't contain your pet's name, must contain a hieroglyph and the phase of the moon. Theo and Priya give useless advice. Dev's **PATIENCE** meter drains to 0%. Then Mochi walks across the keyboard: *"Strong password!"* Dev stares at the cat. *"Please confirm your password."* Mochi yawns. Hard cut to black.

**YouTube title:** When IT says "just update your password" 🔐😭 #corporatelife
