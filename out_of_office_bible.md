# OUT OF OFFICE. — YouTube Short Plan

Draft for approval (Nov 25, 2026). Built from the user's five-scene script, with the BUSY. cast and look. 9:16, ~55 s.

## Overview

**OUT OF OFFICE.** Dev finally takes a day off: beach chair, sunglasses, coconut. Then his phone rings: MANAGER CALLING. The servers are down and, apparently, only Dev can save the company. He fixes everything from his beach chair, asks for a day off next week, and gets "let's discuss that in Monday's meeting." He throws the laptop in his bag and walks into the ocean: "Next vacation… somewhere with NO WIFI!" End screen: **WORK-LIFE BALANCE: 404 NOT FOUND! 😂**

**YouTube title:** When your manager calls you on vacation 🏖️😂 #corporatelife
