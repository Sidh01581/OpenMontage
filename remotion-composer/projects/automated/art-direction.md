# AUTOMATED. — art direction

**Design read:** a sitcom cold open at Dev's desk. A cute desk robot out-works Dev, an email counter climbs out of control, and the manager keeps the robot instead of Dev.

- **Inherited (approved):** BUSY./PASSWORD NIGHTMARE. desk world; DevSeated with the natural posture (now also in the phone pose); Dev and Theo from their approved reference sheets; Priya bust rig from WE NEED TO TALK.; vertical camera and captions. 6 px INK outline, flat cel colour.
- **New for this piece:** the AutoWork AI robot (robot.tsx) and its reference sheet; DevStand with posable arms (WHAT?!, point, slump, mop); TheoCoffee; PriyaReach (phone alert, pat); robot in Dev's chair; install screen, outbox window, EFFORT and EMAILS SENT tags.
- **Signature device:** `AI · EMAILS SENT: 12 → 500 → 4,800` with the antenna blinking on every email.
- **Colour plan:** warm cream/teal office; teal AutoWork windows; cyan robot face; red phone alert; cooler grade on Priya's verdict; warm light on the robot in Dev's chair.
- **Motion:** slow pushes, hard cuts every 1–3 s, shake on BZZT and WHAT?!, hard cut to black at 58.4 s.
- **Layout:** 9:16; faces and captions in the middle 60%; captions at y 1450; Theo and Priya above the cubicle partition. Characters look where they face, never at the camera.
