# BUSY. — art direction

**Design read:** a mock-documentary sitcom: Theo narrates while Dev performs busyness. Every line gets one visual gag, and a tag proves the busiest man in the office does nothing.

- **Inherited (approved):** LATER rigs (Theo head), WE NEED TO TALK. office, Priya, vertical camera and captions; SURE. Dev head. 6 px INK outline, flat cel color.
- **New for this piece:** Dev full body (speed-walk FK rig) and seated desk rig (IK arms); coffee, paper stack, watch, phone; coworker heads; the office aisle; Dev's desk; monitor window cascade; cat-video feed; selfie POV; Employee of the Month frame.
- **Signature device:** the BUSY tag: `STEPS ↑ · COFFEE ↑ · WORK DONE: 0`. It flips to `LOOKING BUSY: 1000%` only while the manager is present.
- **Color plan:** cream/teal office with a mint tag; blue monitor glow for the computer scenes; golden grade for the slow-mo hero walk; navy and gold for the end screen.
- **Motion:** tracking shots for the walk, camera push on every shot, hard cuts every 1.6–2.3 s, flash cut into the end screen, hard cut to black at 41.0 s.
- **Layout:** 9:16; faces and captions inside the middle 60%; nothing important below y 1536. Dev's eyes always look ahead, never at the camera.
