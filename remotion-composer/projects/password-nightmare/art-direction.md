# THE PASSWORD NIGHTMARE. — art direction

**Design read:** a deadpan sitcom escalation. Dev against a polite, merciless password box. Every new rule costs him visible PATIENCE; the cat solves it by accident.

- **Inherited (approved):** BUSY. desk world and DevSeated rig (now with the approved natural posture: flexible neck, relaxed shoulders); SURE. DevHead; WE NEED TO TALK. Priya, vertical camera and captions; LATER. Mochi. 6 px INK outline, flat cel color.
- **New for this piece:** password window (show-password field, growing red rule list, green success state), expiry popup, PATIENCE tag, Dev facedown pose, Mochi walk cycle and yawn, Theo from his new reference sheet (hair without spike, card that turns with the torso), Dev/Theo reference sheets.
- **Signature device:** the red rule list that keeps growing under the field, plus `DEV · PATIENCE: 100% → 0%`.
- **Color plan:** cool office cream/teal; corporate-blue password window; red flash + shake on every rejection; green flash on "Strong password!"; black at the end.
- **Motion:** slow pushes on every shot, hard cuts every 1–3 s, screen shake on rejections, straight cut (no tilted in-between) when Dev pops up, hard cut to black at 44.5 s.
- **Layout:** 9:16; faces and captions in the middle 60%; captions at y 1450; meter at y 270 in screen shots. Characters look where they face, never at the camera.
