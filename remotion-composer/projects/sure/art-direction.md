# SURE. — art direction

**Design read:** a sitcom cold open where the joke is a pile-up: every yes is visible and countable (sticky notes on Theo, copies of Sure), then one proud wrong "NO!" lands in silence.

- **Inherited (approved):** LATER rigs (Theo head, Mochi), WE NEED TO TALK. office, Priya, vertical camera and captions; 6 px INK outline, flat cel color.
- **New for this piece:** Sure, a lime blob with a permanent grin, endless nod and thumbs-up, coral rim (never LATER magenta or What-If violet). It multiplies instead of growing. Dev: curly red hair, freckles, green sweater.
- **Signature device:** the freeze: every Sure stops mid-nod on the record scratch (shot 15).
- **Color plan:** cream/teal office with lime accents -> sticky-note pastels for the yes-spiral -> navy night with one warm lamp -> night-window blues -> soft gold morning.
- **Motion:** camera push on every shot, endless nod cycles, hard cuts every 1.25-3 s, a mid-word hard cut to black at the end.
- **Layout:** 9:16; faces and captions inside the middle 60%; nothing below y 1536.
