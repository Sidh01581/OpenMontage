# WE NEED TO TALK. — art direction

**Design read:** deadpan sitcom cold open in the LATER. vector look, phone-first: big readable faces, big captions, the worry creature as the visible joke.

- **Inherited (approved):** LATER rigs (Theo head, Mochi), 6 px INK outline, flat cel color, light layers carry mood.
- **New for this piece:** What-If, a boiling violet scribble ball with one spiral eye and a teal rim (never LATER magenta); its size is the scoreboard. Priya: square bob and rectangular glasses against Theo's round shapes.
- **Signature device:** the balloon pop of What-If into confetti light in shot 16, the single brightest frame.
- **Color plan:** cream/teal office -> violet dreams -> navy night -> washed-out grey morning -> gold twist -> navy button.
- **Motion:** every shot has a camera move plus secondary motion; hard cuts every 1.5-3 s; true silence before the twist.
- **Layout:** 9:16, faces and captions inside the middle 60%, nothing below y 1536.
