# OUT OF OFFICE. — art direction

**Design read:** a narrated sitcom sequel to BUSY.: paradise, one phone call, and the office reaching Dev on a beach chair. A VACATION meter drains with every line of the call.

- **Inherited (approved):** BUSY. cast and tag style, Dev's head (SURE.), Theo (LATER), Priya + her office (WE NEED TO TALK.), vertical camera and captions. 6 px INK outline, flat cel color.
- **New for this piece:** beach set (waves, palm, umbrella, crab), Dev's vacation outfit (coral Hawaiian shirt, teal shorts, flip-flops, perspective sunglasses), reclined beach-chair rig, laptop, beach bag, walk to the sea, waist-deep front bust; MANAGER CALLING screen, office alarm, status board, server room, IT department, laptop screen.
- **Character rules (user notes):** Dev's head faces the same way as his body and sits upright on his neck; the neck comes out of the shirt collar; his hands never touch his face (phone held out on speaker, coconut below the chin); in side view the far arm stays behind the body.
- **Signature device:** the VACATION meter: 100% → 60% → 30% → 10% → WORK MODE: ON → hope rises to 70% → crashes to 0% → VACATION: 404.
- **Color plan:** sunny beach (turquoise, sand, coral); office emergency with a red alarm wash; green when the servers return; navy 404 end card.
- **Motion:** camera push on every shot, hard cuts every 0.8–3 s, flash into the end screen, cut to black at 56.58 s.
- **Layout:** 9:16; faces and captions inside the middle 60%; nothing important below y 1536.
