# QUICK SYNC. — YouTube Short Plan

Draft for approval (Nov 25, 2026). Same Story Short format as BUSY. (9:16, ~44 s, comedy). Same cast.

## Overview

**QUICK SYNC.** A calendar ping: "URGENT: Quick sync (60 min)." Priya promises it will be quick. It is not. The agenda is "discuss the agenda", everyone who's worked together for three years introduces themselves, and the screen share shows only Priya's desktop. Theo's **ENERGY** meter drains as the clock spins, while Dev looks extremely engaged (he's playing solitaire). Forty minutes later the big update arrives: the printer is fixed. Theo: "That could have been an email." Priya: "Great point, Theo! Let's schedule a meeting to discuss that." *Ping:* "Follow-up: Quick sync (90 min)." Theo face-plants. Hard cut to black.

**YouTube title:** This meeting could've been an email 📧😂 #corporatelife
